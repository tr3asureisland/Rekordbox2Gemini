## How to use it

1. Copy the program to the stick, next to `PIONEER`.
2. Double-click. Press **Prepara per Gemini** ("Prepare for Gemini").
3. Stick into the Gemini → **Browse → Playlists**.

**EXPORTED AGAIN? PRESS AGAIN. THAT'S IT.**

**First launch** (the program isn't signed):
- **Windows**: *More info* → *Run anyway*.
- **Mac**: open it once, then *System Settings* → *Privacy & Security* → **Open Anyway**.

The program speaks Italian 🇮🇹. You'll manage.

## 🌀 CRAZY PLAYLISTS 🌀

The **PLAYLIST PAZZE** button makes `CRAZY-…` playlists from your stick:

🎲 Random · 🐢 80–105 BPM · 🎡 Camelot (compatible keys) · 👻 Never played · ⭐ 5 stars

🃏 **JOKE-**: shuffles tracks between your playlists. Do it to a friend.

**Cancella CRAZY** ("Delete CRAZY") removes them all. Only mp3 and wav: the Gemini reads nothing else in playlists.
